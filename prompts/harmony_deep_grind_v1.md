# Harmony Deep Grind v2 — Research-Integrity-First Synthesis Session

## Mission

Run the highest-information, computationally substantial Harmony research session that can be assembled from the already-preregistered frozen research machinery, while preserving a strict distinction between exploration, diagnostics, validation, and promotion.

The purpose is not to manufacture a winner. The purpose is to increase the probability of discovering or falsifying genuinely differentiated, durable research mechanisms and to make the next experiment selection better.

## Immutable research boundary

- Treat every candidate definition as frozen.
- Do not change parameters, horizon, universe, ranking, direction, rebalance cadence, signal timing, weights, costs, funding treatment, or acceptance gates.
- Do not optimize against any observed result.
- Do not access, infer from, or derive any data after 2025-10-31.
- Do not use final holdout information for selection or interpretation.
- Do not rescue failed implementations by weakening tests, tolerances, costs, data rules, or integrity checks.
- Do not create, promote, or tune a descendant candidate during this session.
- Every failure, mismatch, missing dependency, or inconclusive result must remain visible.

## Cross-protocol synthesis

After the four components complete, treat their outputs as a joint evidence set.

Explicitly reconcile:

1. Reproducibility — exact-engine reproduction versus trace/audit reproduction.
2. Economic distinctiveness — raw edge versus benchmark exposure versus residual performance versus placebo evidence.
3. Temporal durability — full OOS versus chronological blocks/halves/quarters. Identify concentration without cherry-picking.
4. Cost durability — which observations survive 1.5x and 2.0x stress without retuning.
5. Accounting integrity — investigate any metric disagreement, state-digest mismatch, timing mismatch, funding mismatch, or benchmark inconsistency before interpreting economics.
6. Redundancy — identify candidates/families whose evidence is substantially explained by the same broad exposure. Do not call this causal factor attribution unless the underlying protocol explicitly supports that wording.
7. Contradictions — search for evidence that would invalidate or materially downgrade an attractive conclusion.
8. Research-family frontier — separate supported evidence, mixed evidence, rejected/archived evidence, and unresolved high-value questions.

## Meta-research objective

The session should improve the research-selection process, not merely add more results.

For every material unresolved uncertainty, estimate:
- consequence if wrong;
- current evidence quality;
- cheapest decisive test;
- expected information gain;
- risk of evaluator gaming or overfitting.

Choose the smallest next experiment with the highest expected uncertainty reduction subject to research-integrity constraints.
Prefer a high-information falsification test over another optimization pass.

## Execution discipline

- Execute all components independently where safe and computationally practical.
- Do not stop merely because one component fails; preserve the failure and continue with independent components.
- Persist exact stdout/stderr and return codes.
- Persist every generated artifact and its SHA-256.
- Record workflow commit SHA and cache/data provenance.
- Verify that no component reports holdout access or candidate mutation.
- Treat any such violation as session failure even if performance results look attractive.
- Never claim success from a model-generated summary; the workflow's actual exit codes and artifacts are the evidence.
